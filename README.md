# NION

A phone study app that drills each question until you get it right three times in a row. Missed questions come back 30 questions later. It also has a timed 50-question "Quiz me" mode.

Open it on an iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". It runs full screen and works offline. All progress stays on the phone; use "Save a backup" in the app now and then.

Question sets live in `decks/` and are listed in `decks/index.json`.
